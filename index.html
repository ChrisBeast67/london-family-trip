🏰

Christ Church, Oxford

Planned · Thursday 31 Dec. Visit this famous Harry Potter filming location. The Bodley Staircase appeared in the first film; the Great Hall inspired the Hogwarts dining hall design (the film dining scenes used a studio set). Self-guided multimedia tickets are released each Friday at about 10:00am for the following week. For 31 Dec, the expected release is Friday 25 Dec at about 10:00am — but Christmas Day may affect the release, so confirm with the college. Christmas vacation advance prices are £22.95 per adult and £17.50 per child (5–17), if those rates apply at checkout.

A few helpful reminders

Little tips for winter

🧣 Dress warmly

Late December can be cold, windy and wet. Bring layers, waterproof jackets and comfortable walking shoes.

🚇 Leave a buffer

Central London can be busy. Check live Tube and bus information and leave extra time for connections.

🎫 Check before you go

Opening hours and services can change over Christmas and New Year. Recheck official websites close to each date.
